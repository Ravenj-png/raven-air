# 🌬️ Air System Coolers – Corporate Website

A modern, responsive corporate website for **Air System Coolers Ltd**, Uganda’s premier partner for industrial and domestic Mechanical, Electrical, and Fire Protection (MEP) systems.

---

## 📖 About Air System Coolers

Air System Coolers Ltd is an ISO 9001 certified engineering firm with over 25 years of experience across East Africa. The company specializes in:

- **HVAC & Air Conditioning** – Design, installation, and maintenance of VRF systems, chiller plants, and ventilation solutions.
- **Fire Fighting Systems** – Industrial and domestic fire protection, including sprinklers, alarms, and suppression systems.
- **Power Systems** – High and low voltage power distribution, transformers, generators, and backup power solutions.
- **Consultancy Services** – Expert guidance on HVAC selection, energy efficiency, and maintenance planning.

### Featured Clients

- China Communications Construction Co.
- Agago Hydro Power Station
- Electoral Commission Printery
- Military Airdrome Nakasongola
- Busia Market Complex

---

## 🌟 Features

- **Live Weather Dashboard** – Real‑time weather conditions for Kampala, Jinja, Gulu, and Mbarara.
- **Service Showcase** – Detailed breakdown of mechanical, electrical, and fire protection capabilities.
- **Certifications Display** – ISO 9001:2015, NFPA, ASHRAE, AMCA, and CFPS certifications.
- **Partner & Client Logos** – Trusted partners and clients across Uganda.
- **Featured Projects** – Portfolio of completed projects.
- **Technical Insights** – Expert advice on HVAC efficiency and safety standards.
- **Contact & Consultation** – Free consultation request form.
- **Responsive Design** – Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile.
